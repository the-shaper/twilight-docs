## Founders Price

The Founders Price is given to the first clients in Anything You Want. It's the lowest price our services will ever have. After that, the minimum price, along with each addition will have a cost multiplier x 1.618.

### Batch Structure

- **Batch 1 (3 seats)**: Current pricing
- **Batch 2**: Batch 1 pricing x 1.618
- **Batch 3**: Batch 2 pricing x 1.618

### Founders Price Details

**When does it expire?**
For Anything You Want, the membership-based service, the service expires only if you cancel your membership.

**Do I keep it forever if I'm one of the first 3 members?**
No, but a 20% lifetime "gratitude" discount may be given to eligible "founding" clients on all of our services. What makes you eligible? Proof of good intent and proof of respect. We will evaluate the fruitfulness of each collaborative relationship and decide based on that.

**What happens if I pause - do I lose it?**
If you pause Anything You Want, we will give a 3 month "grace period". Should you choose to return within this timeframe, you'll keep your Founders Price price.

## Price Structure

|            | Base       | Cost / mo   | Upgrade      | Cost / mo     |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------ | ------------- |
| **Craft**  | Initiation | $1,990$ USD | Dreamcasting | + $3,333$ USD |
| **Energy** | Generator  | Included    | Reactor      | + $1,618$ USD |

Upgrades are optional to each section (Craft and Energy). So you might not need the Dreamcasting skill upgrade but you need the Reactor energy, or vice versa.

### Future Member Pricing

Batch 1 are the first 3 founding members, matching our current max of 3 seats.

Once these spaces are occupied, the next batches will be counted by 3s again. Next 3 members (4-6) get Batch 2 pricing. Then, next 3 (7-9) members get Batch 3 pricing. After that, pricing will be based on demand.

We plan to keep the limit of 3 seats until we are sure that the bandwidth will come with increases in quality of delivery, rather than the opposite.

## Payment Methods

- **Standard method**: Stripe
- **Preferred method**: Bitcoin (BTC)

### Bitcoin Benefits

If you pre-pay 3 months of Anything You Want with BTC, you'll save 20% of the total package cost.

## POW! Grant

POW! Stands for "Proof of Work!". It gives you an extra 20% discount when pre-paying 3 months with Bitcoin.

We made this initiative because:

1. We are launching with zero (0) portfolio
2. We want to work only with people with aligned pursuits. Decentralization and self-sovereignty are key to us.

### Grant Details

**What happens after the 3-month prepayment period?**
You can renew and keep the same initial price (based on total dollar amount -20%)

**Can I combine POW! Grant with Founders Price?**
Absolutely. It gets combined by default.

## Refund Policy

**Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?**
If after your first month, you genuinely feel dissatisfied with the work we produced, we will give you the next month for free.

**Is there a trial period?**
We don't offer trial periods as our current base pricing is already subsidized.

## Membership Management

### Pausing or Canceling

Yes. It is important that you cancel your membership at least 2 weeks before the end of your current billing period. This time is important to us for two things:

1. Preparing all the work generated for you to be delivered as orderly as possible
2. To use the time to notify people who might be on the waiting list, so they have time to consider and prepare

**What happens if I don't notify my cancellation on time?**
Failing to notify within the first two weeks of your current billing period will result in a $600 charge against the final delivery of the generated files.

**What happens to my Founders Price if I pause?**
You get to keep your Founders Price for up to 3 months of inactivity.

**What happens to work-in-progress if I cancel?**
It gets prepared and delivered to you as-is, or up to the state it got until the last checkpoint in the billing period.
