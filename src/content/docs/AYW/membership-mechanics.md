## Application Process

### How does the application process work?

**What do you evaluate?**

- Affinity: Do you work in an industry or field where we genuinely feel we can add value to?
- Compatibility: Are our work dynamics, pace of work and general processes complatible with yours? If not, what would need to change?
- Ownership and communication: Are you able to articulate and clearly communicate your objectives?

**How long until I hear back?**
Around 24-48 hours from submission time.

**What if I'm rejected?**
We will notify you too and we will explain why.

## What Makes a Good Application

Be clear about what you do and what you are up to. This is important for us because it helps us see if/how we can be most useful to you.

### Who is this service NOT for?

- Politicians and political activists
- Committees (Or groups where there is no head decision-maker)
- People trying to save a bad product with nice "aesthetics"

## Founders at the Fringe

### What does "founders at the fringe" mean?

We see Founders at the Fringe as the people who operate at the margins of knowledge, culture and even society. We think of those who feel a sense of "dislocation" from what they see or where they are, because they entertain ideas few people like or care to entertain, yet they are obsessive about some of these ideas and are obstinate enough to push until they see them realized.

It is also the people who don't necessarily live near hubs or melting pots, but build and see themselves as citizens of the world.

### Why is this important to us?

Because people like us build for people like us.

**Can established companies apply?**
Established companies are welcome to apply. However, there are a few caveats:

- We believe that "A camel is a horse designed by committee."

**Is this only for startups?**
Not necessarily, but it is aimed at visionaries and risk-takers. Most of which found startups.

## Seat Availability

### What happens if all 3 seats are full?

**How does the waitlist work?**
New seats will open whenever a client pauses or cancels their membership

**Do I get notified when a seat opens?**
Yes, you will be notified around 2 weeks in advance of a position opening.

## Membership Changes

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my membership?

Yes, you can!

This can only be done up to 3 days before renewal. If a month's period is already going, the downgrade will apply until the next reset.

## Commitment Period

### What's the minimum commitment period?

You are able to subscribe for only 1 month, but you need to cancel at least 2 weeks in advance before the end the ongoing month.

### Why 2 weeks notice?

We need time:

- To prepare all the output we produced for you so you can have a full delivery at the end of the month
- To give our waitlist a heads-up so your previously used seat doesn't stay empty for a month.

### What happens if I don't cancel at least 2 weeks in advance?

- We will charge a $600 recovery fee.
- We will deliver the output produced during your time with us within 2 weeks after your period's end.

### Is there a discount for longer commitments?

- You can save an extra 20% if you pre-pay 3 months with Bitcoin.

## Pausing Membership

### If I pause, do I lose my spot?

Momentarily, yes. Someone from the waitlist will take your seat.
However, if you paused without canceling, you will be prioritized and be notified first when a new seat opens.

Pausing can be up to 12 months.

## Membership Tiers

### Current Structure

We maintain a limit of 3 seats to ensure quality delivery doesn't suffer as we grow. This allows us to:

- Provide focused attention to each member
- Maintain high quality standards
- Ensure bandwidth matches delivery quality

### Future Scaling

As we grow and prove our ability to maintain quality while increasing capacity, we may:

- Open additional seats in batches of 3
- Adjust pricing based on demand
- Implement new tiers or services

## Billing and Payments

### Billing Cycle

- Monthly cycle starts upon first payment
- Billing is automated and must be linked to a card or bank account via Stripe
- If billing attempts fail for 1 week, a grace period of 1 week is given to Anything You Want seat holders

### Payment Methods

- **Standard**: Stripe (credit card, bank transfer)
- **Preferred**: Bitcoin (with 20% discount for 3-month prepayment)

### Failed Payments

If billing attempts fail:

- 1 week grace period is provided
- Service may be suspended if payment issues persist
- Reinstatement requires settling outstanding balance
