Anything You Want offers two energy tiers designed to match different project needs and working styles: **Generator** and **Reactor**.

## Generator

Generator is our standard energy tier that provides consistent, reliable creative production capacity.

### What's Included

- **1 task per checkpoint**
- Standard working hours (9:00-18:00 PST)
- 1 remote viewing session per week
- Standard response times
- Basic emergency support (best effort)

### Best For

- Founders with steady, predictable project needs
- Teams who prefer regular, paced delivery
- Projects that benefit from thoughtful iteration
- Budget-conscious members

## Reactor

Reactor is our high-energy tier designed for urgent, intensive, or parallel project needs.

### What's Included

- **2 tasks per checkpoint**
- Extended working hours (90 min before/after standard hours)
- 2 remote viewing sessions per week
- Priority response times
- Dedicated emergency support

### What Exactly Doubles with Reactor?

**Is it 2 tasks per checkpoint or 4 checkpoints per week?**
It's two tasks per checkpoint

**Can I split tasks across different skill areas?**
Yes. Tasks can be totally unrelated

## Task Management

### Using Reactor for Single Goals

**Can I use Reactor to speed up one big goal or must they be two separate goals running parallel?**

Yes, you can absolutely do this. However, we do have one belief in regards to creativity:
Some processes can't be sped up. Some processes take time to make sense. Sometimes you will find your projects new Name in 5 minutes, sometimes it will take you 5 years.

For us, the key to navigating this is to know when a project needs time and when it can be sped up BUT never stopping.

All this to say that sometimes, doubling the bandwidth might give you a "higher" volume of deliverables, (such as explorations of a logotype), but it won't necessarily increase the quality. In such cases, having a "next most important task" is very useful. That way, we never stop.

## Extended Connection

### What does "extended connection" mean in Reactor?

In short, it means extended –and more immediate– access to us.

**Specifically:**

- 2 remote viewings per week
- Extended hours: open 90 min before opening time and 90 min after closing time
- Better response times: With the assumption that Reactor projects are "high energy" by nature, we prioritize messaging and phone calls with Reactor customers.

## Emergency Support

### Generator Emergency Support

The "Generator" tier DOES NOT support emergencies, however, if helping is within our possibilities and reach, we will do so.

### Reactor Emergency Support

The "Reactor" Energy tier supports emergencies

**Is there an emergency contact?**
Yes.

**What's considered "urgent"?**
A deadline must be met (crunch time) or an unexpected event with critical consequences arise.

## Who Should Choose Reactor?

### Is Reactor worth it for solo founders?

**If you are on a tight deadline and have clear goals that you need to delegate, absolutely.**
**If your deadline is not as tight and need more time to clarify your goals, Generator should be more than enough.**

### When should I upgrade to Reactor?

- When you feel like your business needs higher output on our side
- When you're facing critical deadlines
- When you need to run parallel projects simultaneously
- When you require more immediate access and support

## Pricing Impact

The Reactor upgrade adds **$1,618 USD per month** to your base subscription.

This upgrade is independent of the Craft tier (Initiation vs Dreamcasting), so you can have:

- Initiation + Generator
- Initiation + Reactor
- Dreamcasting + Generator
- Dreamcasting + Reactor

## Practical Examples

### Generator Use Cases

- Building a brand identity over 2-3 months
- Developing a website with regular iterations
- Creating marketing materials on a steady schedule
- Ongoing design support for established businesses

### Reactor Use Cases

- Launching a product under tight deadline
- Running multiple concurrent projects
- Rapid prototyping and iteration
- Emergency brand pivots or crisis response

## Switching Between Tiers

You can upgrade or downgrade your energy tier with 3 days' notice before renewal. This allows you to:

- Upgrade for intensive project phases
- Downgrade during quieter periods
- Optimize your investment based on current needs
